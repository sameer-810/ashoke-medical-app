Delete your Ashoke Medical account

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Ashoke Medical is the billing and stock software used by the staff of Ashoke Medical & General Stores. This page explains how a staff member's account is deleted, what is removed, and what the store keeps.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to the app and open Profile.
  2. Scroll to Delete account and enter your password.
  3. Confirm. The account is deleted at once and cannot be restored.
If you cannot sign in, ask the store's Admin to remove your account under Team & Access. The store's only Admin account cannot be deleted, because nobody else could manage the team; the store can create another Admin first.

What is deleted

Deleted Kept, and why
Your sign-in
Your name, email and mobile number
Your password
Your signed-in devices
Your name on the activity log 		The bills, purchases and stock entries you made stay on the store's records without your name. The store is legally required to keep its GST invoices and its Schedule H register, generally for six years.

How long it takes

Deletion in the app is immediate. Backups of the store's data are kept for up to 30 days, so a deleted account can remain in a backup for that long before it is overwritten.

Questions

Speak to the Admin at Ashoke Medical & General Stores. See also the Privacy Policy.
